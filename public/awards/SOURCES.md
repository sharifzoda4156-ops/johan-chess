# Profile achievements
Rules: https://github.com/Treasure-Chess/chess-achievements/blob/master/src/achievementsList.js (MIT, Nifty Chess, Inc.). Source IDs retained in achievement-core.js. A supported subset is implemented independently using chess.js; translated labels and local persistence are Johan Chess adaptations. Standard completed games only; rating-based awards require imported Chess.com opponent rating. No online tournament awards or fictional rankings. Icons: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/main/icons (ISC; award, medal, trophy; stroke color adapted). Full licenses are bundled. Lichess tournament trophies are not reused as local achievements because their official criteria require Lichess competition data.
